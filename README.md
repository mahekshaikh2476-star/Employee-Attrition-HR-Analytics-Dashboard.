# Employee Attrition & HR Analytics Dashboard



## Project Overview

The Employee Attrition & HR Analytics Dashboard is a workforce analytics project designed to help HR teams and business leaders understand employee turnover, workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, engagement levels, and retention opportunities.

This project leverages Python and Power BI to transform HR data into actionable insights that support strategic workforce planning and data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges organizations face. High turnover leads to increased recruitment costs, loss of productivity, knowledge gaps, and reduced employee morale.

Organizations need a centralized analytics solution to:

- Monitor employee attrition trends
- Identify departments with high turnover
- Understand factors affecting employee retention
- Measure employee satisfaction and engagement
- Support workforce planning initiatives

This dashboard provides a comprehensive HR analytics solution to address these challenges.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Measure workforce performance metrics
- Identify high-risk employee groups
- Evaluate employee satisfaction and engagement
- Assess overtime and promotion impact on attrition
- Support employee retention strategies
- Enable data-driven HR decision-making
